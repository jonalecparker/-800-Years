## One wall system, no grid (2026-07-27)

> **⚠ Rebuild decided (2026-07-29): this whole system moves to a node/edge graph** — see `Docs/WallGraphRebuild.md`. The inference machinery below (bands, grazes, gates, IsBlocked, ignore lists) is scheduled for deletion; the meshing, wedge rule, vertical math, and assists survive. Do NOT extend the junction/occupancy machinery — fix-forward only if something blocks a playtest.

- **Every wall is a `SplineWall`** — a straight wall is a bulge-0 spline committed by the drag tool; a plain click is a token 10cm chord the end extensions grow into a short stub. `PlacedPiece`, per-cell placement, `SnapToGrid`, and `GridVisualizer` are all gone — **placement is free**. Alignment comes from assists: endpoint chaining (0.75m), drag length stepping **relative to the anchor** (`lengthStep` 0.5m; direction is freeform, Shift snaps it to `angleStep` 15°, Alt frees everything), distance guides, and the **Offset tool** (BuildMenu button: click a wall → scroll steps a locked parallel distance, cursor side picks the side, click lays the copy — `SplineWall.OffsetCurve`). `gridSize` now just means wall thickness / target section length.
- Occupancy is **physics overlap**, implemented once in `SplineWall.IsBlocked`, and it only refuses a wall whose **centerline** reaches another wall's body (slim thickness extent) — a wall merely leaning its face into a neighbour is legal and gets **fused flush** by graze clipping instead.
- **Walls never intersect (2026-07-29)**: a band the sweep passes THROUGH (`Band.through` — it opens after the body starts AND closes before the body ends) **refuses its span red** instead of cutting — crossing a wall means drawing two walls, one on each side (the clear side spans still place in one gesture; the refused middle never does). Cut geometry survives only for **terminal burials** — a band containing an END (began before the body did, or outlives the body's end: contact T-fuses, collinear gap bridges, redraws entering through a cap) — and grazes. Junction cuts themselves are unchanged: where a terminal band sits at ≥10° (`FindJunctionBands`, one band **per host wall** — bands overlap at multi-wall junctions), interior sections are dropped and every kept piece carries the applicable hosts' **face planes** (`cutPoints`/`cutNormals`, inset 1.5cm into the host to avoid z-fighting). On **curved hosts** the raycast plane is only a tangent — `FaceRecession` measures the host's true face **analytically** (its Bézier offset by half thickness; the box colliders are useless for this — cracks and interior caps) across the cut's lateral span and sinks the plane by the deepest recession (cap 0.25m), so flat caps bury inside curved masonry instead of leaving a daylight wedge. Flat hosts measure zero — the inset stays exactly 1.5cm. Mesh rows clip their cross-section against each plane (`ClipRow`), so cut edges follow host faces exactly — flush butt caps at 90°, clean diagonals at shallow angles. Cutting is asymmetric and stable via `buildOrder` — newer cuts against older, never the reverse.
- **Graze lanes** (side-offset scans, angle-free) catch what the centerline misses: a face dipping into a host mid-run or a wall leaning alongside one — the face plane comes from a sideways raycast and clips the sliver flush (near-parallel walls **fuse** instead of interpenetrating). Cut-plane ride-along is bounded to 3m of the band (legit shallow-angle reach is ~2.8m; a plane parallel to the run would otherwise shave the flank off every downstream piece). Extension crumbs severed from the body by a band (chained-corner overshoot) are dropped in `EmitPiece`.
- **Contact ⇒ connect, gaps stay gaps**: end extensions only fuse on contact (`EndExtension` probes 0.4 + 0.2 radius = 0.6 from the endpoint — just past the 0.5 default cap). A touching or flank-snapped end overshoots into the wall and the cut planes trim it back flush (the T-join); a visible gap is never bridged — reaching a wall at distance is the end snap's job, not a reach-back's.
- **Snapped ends ⇒ nodes, and the node owns the joint (2026-07-28)**: walls stay straight and the turning lives in the joint. A node end (`nodeAtStart/nodeAtEnd`) caps flat exactly ON the shared point — end extension stands down, and the junction scan starts a sample step past the post radius (partners *touch* at the node by construction; touch must not read as burial, and a boundary sample grazing a partner's box corner once dropped a bridge's last half meter). The **`WallNode`** at the point renders the joint: an implicit round post (radius = half thickness) that every member's faces are tangent to, meshed as only the **exposed arc wedges** between adjacent members (sort inward bearings; each gap exposes gap−180°). Members are **end members** (wall caps on the point) or **flank members** (a T at any angle: the point sits ON the host's face, host joins as a half-plane — inward bearing = flank normal into its body — geometry untouched, no flags). Collinear butt ⇒ no post at all, L ⇒ quarter barrel, hairpin ⇒ rounded tip, 90° tee ⇒ flush flat butt, 8° tee ⇒ one shoulder arc — one rule, any angle, any member count, zero cut geometry at joints. **Same-node members stand down for each other everywhere** (`ignoreWalls` through `BuildSpecs`→bands/grazes/`IsBlocked`): overlap with a member is legal *buried masonry* (`SectionSpec.buriedInMember`), which is what makes sub-10° tees and near-member node joins work — the 10° gate now only governs true crossings. The one refusal left: a wall whose EVERY piece is buried in its members is a redraw along one — blocked red wholesale. `WallNode.Connect` (commit-side) is **multi-pass and order-critical**: flag every wall ending on the point, retract the newly flagged, then rebuild the *other* members — a wall committed a moment ago probed the pre-retraction world. A node losing its second-to-last member dissolves, un-nodes the survivor (end members only), and the delete flow's linked rebuild regrows its cap. Both ends snap: `TryEndSnap` (endpoint + two flanks per end; the old cap candidate died with the overshoot) then `TryFlankSnap` (mid-wall face point, host end zones excluded ±0.9). **Which face**: the far end of a drag takes the face the ANCHOR is on (`from` picks the side); the **anchor** hover (2026-07-29) snaps onto the face on the **cursor's** side — click plants the anchor ON the face, drawing away is the same flank node (this replaced the old lay-flush-alongside hover gesture; parallel runs are the Offset tool's job). A **flank-anchored drag steps its direction from the face normal** (always, `angleStep` 15° — a lazy drag lands exactly 90° flush; Alt frees), and `PendingIgnores` only stands the flank host down when the wall **leaves on its face's side** — a drag across it loses the stand-down and refuses red as the intersection it is. Snap-hover ghosts are **rectangular outward stubs** (`BuildSnappedStubPreview`: token chord leaving the point along `outDir`, node-capped flat ON it — wholly outside the host; the half-buried post marker is gone, and drag-time post cylinders show only at endpoint ends). The curve tool's chord phase must **discard a flank hover pick and re-derive anchor-side** — the hover chose by cursor, and a chord overshooting the host would land on the far face. Known edges: hovering a face 0.75–0.9m from a host end hits neither snap (dead sliver, free anchor); stacked courses / gapped walls at a late-forming node keep their old overshoot caps; a terminating wall cut by an unrelated crossing before reaching its node leaves the wedge arc floating.
- **Tangent-tied connector is Shift-opt-in now**: both drag ends snapped + **Shift** ⇒ `TryTangentControl` auto-curves the wall to leave one host and arrive at the other straight-on (both joints flush end-on fuses); the default is straight + corner nodes. Parallel runs (an S-curve, out of a quadratic's reach) and diverging tangents fall back to straight. Supporting rules the junction machinery leans on: pieces cut by a band pull their occupancy box back 0.3 from the cut (the deliberate burial must not read as a near-parallel duplicate), the angle gate re-judges near-threshold calls against the host's **local tangents** (a tight curve's box yaw turns 20°+ per section — a redraw would read as crossing itself), and only **gate-qualified** bands get the stay-open hysteresis (a parallel band opened in the gate-free end zone dies at the zone boundary, so a redraw entering through a cap refuses red instead of vanishing).
- **Distance guides**: while placing, the square around the ghost (±`guideRange` 5m) splits into four sectors from its corners — ahead/behind/left/right of the run — and each sector draws one line + label (IMGUI) to its closest wall face (`UpdateDistanceGuides` / `SplineWall.ClosestFacePoint`). Max four lines, spacing context in every direction.
- **Refused placement shows red, never vanishes**: previews build with `includeBlocked: true`, so duplicate-blocked pieces carry `SectionSpec.blocked` and tint red (`SetGhostTint`); `SplineWall.Create` skips and disposes blocked specs, and `ActiveDragCount`/commit guards ignore them.
- Connections are **modeled**: `SplineWall.Links` (expanded-box overlap — flush contact, T-joints, and stacked courses all count) is resolved at commit, and the delete tool makes linked survivors `RebuildSections()` — buried stubs retract, junction gaps regrow solid when the host dies. Stacked courses (`isStackedCourse`) and walls with delete holes (`hasGaps`) never rebuild — a rebuild would drop a course to the terrain or resurrect the holes.

## Vertical math is spans, not layers

- `SplineWallSection.bottomY/topY` is the source of truth. Never assume uniform section height or ground at y=0 (terrain exists; cell ground = min of 4 corners, quantized down by `baseStepSize`).
- Bulge (0.5m) and foundation bases (0.5m) are deliberately **stepped** so separately-built structures align — don't make those continuous. Wall height is the exception: it scrolls **smoothly** (Ctrl+scroll, `heightScrollRate` 0.25m/notch, raw ±120 Windows deltas normalized), and alignment comes from snapping instead — a ghost snapped to a wall (end or flank) adopts that wall's exact height via `EffectiveWallHeight` (anchor snap > drag-end snap > hover snap > scrolled height; Alt frees both position and height). A **locked-top host matches by top elevation**, not stored height (`SnapHeightOf` = `fixedTopY` − ground at the snap — stored height means nothing on a slope), and **Ctrl+scroll overrides the match** (`heightOverride`, re-arms when the drag ends or the hover leaves the snap).

## Spline wall geometry

- Section meshes are watertight only because neighbors evaluate the same curve at the same boundary parameter from one shared arc table. Section `tStart/tEnd` can be **outside [0,1]** (end extensions) — use `ExtendedArcAt`/`TFromExtendedArc`, not `ArcAt`/`TAtArc`, for anything touching stored section params.
- Section meshes are runtime-owned (`ownedMesh`, destroyed with the section); preview meshes are cached by input-tuple and destroyed on rebuild — leaks appear fast if commit/cancel paths stop transferring ownership.

## Input arbitration

- The scroll wheel has four meanings: camera dolly (default), wall height (Ctrl), curve bulge (bend phase), offset distance (Offset tool with a source selected). The last two claim the wheel via `GridPlacementSystem.ClaimingScrollWheel`; `FreeFlyCamera` checks it and Ctrl before dollying — any new wheel use must join this arbitration.
- No trigger colliders exist in the build scene anymore (the grid pieces' assist boxes died with them), but raycast/overlap code still filters `isTrigger` — keep those filters when adding new triggers, or placement targeting will misread them.

## Tooling gotchas

- `manage_texture`'s `set_import_settings` MCP action silently drops its payload — set import settings via `execute_code` (editor API) or hand-edit the `.meta`.
- HDRP motion blur smears screenshots taken the frame after a camera teleport — capture twice, keep the second.
- After script edits: `refresh_unity` (compile) + `read_console` for errors before proceeding; play-mode `execute_code` tests that delete-then-rebuild must use `DestroyImmediate` (deferred `Destroy` races validation).
