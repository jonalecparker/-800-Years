# Session — 2026-08-08: real ground, honest planes, and buildings built bottom-up

*One continuous working conversation covering 2026-08-06 → 08-08 — four
arcs in one commit: the Spiš Castle terrain, the Ground tool, the
quarter-grid unification with its readouts, and the full building
rebuild. Plus, at the end, named save slots and a tidier menu. The
earlier arcs are reconstructed here partly from the CLAUDE.md doctrine
written at the time; the later arcs are first-hand.*

## Goal

Near-arc steps 2→3 (`Docs/Roadmap.md`): put the builder on a real hill,
then fix what building there exposed. The exposure was severe — trying
to join a second storey of building B to building A on the hill proved
that derived floor/roof planes can never rendezvous — and the answer
grew into a full rebuild of the room system (`Docs/BuildingRebuild.md`,
agreed and shipped the same day).

## What we did

**1. Real terrain — Spiš Castle, Slovakia** (`TerrainGenerator.cs`,
`Assets/Terrain/SpisTile.bytes`):

- `demHeightmap` TextAsset slot on the generator: raw little-endian
  float32 elevations, 1m grid, row-major from the NW corner
  (ETRS89/UTM34N E 482545..483569, N 5427020..5428044). When set,
  `ApplyDemHeights` overrides the procedural countryside; the tile's own
  min elevation becomes y=0. The procedural path survives untouched for
  test scenes.

**2. The Ground tool and the deviation law** (`TerrainPads.cs` new,
Ground button):

- **THE DEVIATION LAW**: ground may never differ from the *pristine*
  heightmap by more than `MaxDeviation` (3m — user tuned 2→5→3m across
  08-07). Enforced per-sample inside `Reshape` against PRISTINE, not
  current ground, so repeated carving can't creep. The law is the
  pre-economy balance and the siege-era protection (no moats, no
  cliff-carving); buildings never move earth.
- **Path**: drag benches a walkable band between the two ends' ground
  heights; Shift holds the anchor's height (a level path). The drag
  deforms the REAL terrain live and commits on release; Esc restores;
  preview and commit go through the same door so they cannot differ.
  End heights sample the pre-preview snapshot, never live ground — the
  bench deforms the ground under its own ends.
- **Restore**: a hold-and-sweep paint brush that returns ground to
  pristine — inherently abuse-proof, its only power is undoing.
- Earthworks save as **typed operations** replayed in order before
  masonry on load; ops are permanent (Restore is the undo, deleting a
  building doesn't un-dig). Reshaped ground repaints its slope-derived
  grass/dirt alphamap.
- **The one-test doctrine** minted here: any commit-time refusal must be
  warned live by running the SAME test — an approximate lines-only check
  once warned nothing where the commit refused.

**3. The quarter-grid unification and the elevation readouts**
(user's call, after proving floors on the 0.5m grid can never meet
roofs on the free/0.25m dial):

- `CurrentWallHeight` **quantizes to `heightScrollRate`** (0.25m) — the
  dial LANDS on the quarter grid instead of merely moving by it. Slab
  planes seat on the same lattice. Footings stay on the half-metre
  `baseStep` (⊂ the quarter lattice), so every free-built top can meet
  every other by scrolling instead of luck. Snapped adoption still
  copies exact heights — matching a neighbour IS alignment and beats
  the grid.
- Two readouts serve the rendezvous: **`GhostTopY`** (the absolute
  elevation the active ghost's top lands at, set by every preview
  builder from the same numbers the commit writes) and **`InspectLine`**
  (what the cursor is over, as *stored* planes — never hit-point
  geometry, so the cutaway can't misreport).

**4. The building rebuild** (`Docs/BuildingRebuild.md` — brief and ship
on the same day, 08-08): buildings are authored bottom-up from
foundation tiles, walls, floor tiles, and (later) decorative roofs — 
**no plane is ever inferred**. Stored-never-inferred applied to the
vertical axis, finishing what the wall-graph rebuild did to the planar.

- **`SlabTile`** (new): one class, two tools. Square prism, transform
  built, sliced by the cutaway like everything else. Foundation tiles
  seat at the highest ground under them on the quarter grid, skirt over
  dips, snap edge-to-edge and **adopt the host's plane and yaw**; the
  one refusal is a tile mostly buried in a hillside (>50% of samples),
  which is what stops one adopted plane marching up a hill. Floor tiles
  exist only by **connection** — seeded at a wall top at that wall's
  exact plane, or grown off another floor tile. A stairwell is the tile
  you don't place.
- **The kill list executed** — `WallRoom` deleted entirely: the crown
  vote, derived floors and roofs, designate, `CarriedToPlane`, wells,
  `RaiseSills`, the partition tell, the floor step, the
  never-cross-into-a-building landing. The problem class those laws
  policed is now *unrepresentable*: buildings live on slab storeys the
  terrain gesture's per-base planar graph never meets.
- **`Enclosure`** (new, the lean survivor): closing the Room chain
  traces the face and ANNOUNCES it in the BuildLog; roomness becomes
  stored gameplay state when the economy needs it, not before.
- Free wins: building walls bottom exactly at the slab (footing-stepping
  survives only under terrain walls); a doorway's sill is the wall's own
  ground by construction.
- **Save schema Version 2**: rooms out, slab tiles in. Version-1 saves
  refuse cleanly and the old `Saves/castle.json` was deleted — the
  user's call: "keep it simple and just delete the old saves."

**5. Named save slots and the Room category** (this closing window):

- **`CastleSave` slots**: a slot is a file in `Saves/` — the name is the
  file name, the date the write time, nothing stored twice.
  `CurrentSlot` is whatever was last saved or loaded; F5/F9 act on it.
- **The Saves panel** (`BuildMenu`): modal, keyboard-capturing (slot
  names are typed — `Keyboard.onTextInput`, filename-safe chars only,
  the field filter and the commit-time `SanitizeSlot` are the same
  test). Opens prefilled with the current slot; Enter/Save writes;
  click a listed slot to load. `BuildMenu.ModalOpen` is a stand-down
  flag like `WalkMode.Active`: the placement system stands down on it
  and `WalkMode` ignores Tab while it's open. `StandDown` now also
  clears `InspectLine`/`ghostTopY` — walk mode hides the HUD but the
  panel doesn't, and a stale readout would report a dead hover.
- **The Room button is the building category**: its item row holds the
  building tools in build order — Foundation / Wall (the chain gesture)
  / Floor — and opening it arms Foundation, because a building starts
  from one. The top-level Foundation/Floor buttons are gone.

## Decisions & trade-offs

- **The player states planes; the game never intuits them** — the
  user's framing: "letting the player choose where they want between
  stories means we don't have to intuit it which i am getting the
  feeling we can never do perfectly." No separate roof/floor objects
  ("Setting the roof is basically the same thing as setting the floor
  for the story above"), no dual auto+manual roofing.
- **Quarter grid everywhere** rather than exempting wall heights —
  otherwise "it's just too hard to line things up."
- **Terrain walls survive untouched** as curtain walls in the new
  system (user confirmed); support rules (~20m span cap, same-height
  connection) and decorative roofs deferred by the brief, deliberately.
- **Old saves deleted, not migrated.**
- **Slot dates are file write times** — the panel stores nothing the
  Saves folder doesn't already say.

# Session — 2026-08-08 (второй): the hand-test verdict, a found crusher, and slabs you draw

*Same day, second session: the user hand-tested the building rebuild and
filed a bug batch; chasing the worst of it found a frame-loop crusher
that had nothing to do with placement; and the day ended with the slab
half of the building rebuild replaced — square tiles out, drawn polygon
outlines in, at the user's call after feeling both.*

## Goal

Fix what the first hand-test of the building rebuild exposed, then act
on its verdict.

## What we did

**1. Saves panel fixes** (`FreeFlyCamera.cs`, `BuildMenu.cs`,
`CastleSave.cs`):

- WASD no longer flies the camera while typing a slot name —
  `FreeFlyCamera` stands down on `BuildMenu.ModalOpen` (it only checked
  `WalkMode.Active`).
- Each slot row grew a **trashcan** (`CreateSlotDeleteButton`, drawn
  from rects — the built-in font has no glyph) → `CastleSave.DeleteSlot`.
  No confirm; a deleted `CurrentSlot` stays current.

**2. The sinking-foundation bug — found on the third diagnosis**
(`CutawayView.cs`, `CutawayPivot.cs`):

- Symptom: a snapped green ghost, but the clicked tile sat crushed below
  the terrain — only on non-level ground. Two diagnoses were WRONG and
  their fixes reverted/removed at the user's insistence (a click-frame
  re-resolve race — the cache-commit it produced was kept as a plain
  correctness guarantee; then a cursor-slip/cliff-edge theory — "it is
  not a cursor movement issue").
- The real bug: **`CutawayView.Restore` forced `scale.y = 1` on
  everything under `splineParent` every frame, even with no cut
  active.** Walls are world-space meshes on unit transforms and never
  noticed; a tile's height WAS its Y-scale, so every tile taller than a
  metre was crushed about its own middle one frame after commit, while
  the scene-root ghost kept its true shape. The tile's *stored* data was
  right all along — the squash was visual+collider only.
- Fix: `CutawayPivot` caches `authoredScaleY` alongside `authoredY`, and
  all span/slice/restore math multiplies mesh bounds by the authored
  scale. Machine-verified: a 4.25m skirted tile survives ticks uncut,
  slices to the exact cut plane, restores exactly.

**3. Placement fixes that lived a few hours** (tile-era
`GridPlacementSystem`): the mirror-slide fix (`AbutTile`'s slide axis
was derived from the signed normal and ran against the cursor on two of
four edges), the `MaxSkirt` 2m law (user's design: a skirt that can't
find ground within it refuses, red on the ghost), and the
always-aligned snap lattice (slide lands only on centered/corner/
ghost-size-step positions). All correct, all machine-verified — and all
but `MaxSkirt` **died the same evening** when tiles themselves did.

**4. THE OUTLINE REBUILD** — the hand-test verdict: "square tiles over
irregular shape makes it hard to impossible to floor a room… it felt
right when i was able to draw the room shape via the walls." Slabs are
now **drawn polygon outlines**, both tools the chain gesture
(`SlabTile.cs` rewritten, `GridPlacementSystem.cs` slab region
rewritten, `CastleSave.cs` schema v3):

- `SlabTile` is a **polygon prism**: `verts` (world XZ, CCW) +
  planes, ear-clip triangulated (concave OK), world-space mesh on a
  unit transform — the wall convention, so the cutaway needs nothing
  special — with a MeshCollider. Polygon predicates (`SignedArea`,
  `Contains`, `SelfIntersects`, `PolyOverlaps`, `Triangulate`) are
  static on the class, shared by ghost and commit.
- **The gesture**: click corners, close on the first corner, the
  polygon fills as ONE prism. Esc abandons. Ghost = the filled
  tentative polygon (chain closed through the cursor) + corner markers;
  one corner + cursor draws a thin blade.
- **One outline, one plane; the anchor states it** — seated from its
  ground on the quarter grid, or adopted from a snapped outline —
  **plus a new Ctrl+scroll plane dial in ¼m steps** (foundations only).
  The two laws (>50% burial, 2m skirt) sample the polygon and return
  the refusal STRING the red ghost and the click both use. A 20m square
  on a 6m slope refuses at every plane — the design says terrace.
- **Floors**: anchor on a wall top or floor edge; after that the cursor
  maps onto the plane itself, so the outline crosses air — a stairwell
  is **drawn around**. Corners snap to wall nodes carrying masonry at
  the plane (flooring a room = clicking its corners) and to floor
  outlines.
- **Save schema v3**: `SlabData` carries the outline; **v2 square tiles
  migrate on load** as the 4-corner outlines they were (verified with a
  synthetic v2 file, exact corners). The user's `Terraformed` slot
  loads.
- Died with the tiles: R rotation, the size dial, tile abutment (and
  with it the morning's aligned-slide lattice), the frame-stamped
  cache-commit (a chain click commits the same-frame resolved point).

## Decisions & trade-offs

- **Tiles replaced, not kept alongside** — a square is a 4-corner
  outline; two authoring models for one object kind wasn't worth it.
- **One plane per outline + dial + terrace** — the height question a
  big footprint raises is answered by the laws refusing, not by the
  slab bending. Historically right (Spiš is terraced) and it keeps
  planes stated.
- **Floors draw around stairwells** — omission survives as shape. The
  trace-stamp fill (Enclosure → stored polygon, for curved rooms) is
  the agreed second tool, deferred until the outline is judged.
- The burial depth-variant experiment was reverted at the user's call
  ("that was not the right fix please revert") — the >50%-area rule
  stands until a playtest verdict.

## Bugs hit

- `BuildPrism`'s side quads wound inward as first written — caught by
  eye before compile.
- Grep display artifact noted: `//` sometimes renders as `\ ` in
