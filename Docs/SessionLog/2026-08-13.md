## Goal

Territories: castle sites → lordships → land types per territory,
one contiguous town per manor, ownership by lordship. Then, mid-session,
the user's castle directive: computer-controlled castles as real

## What we did

**1. Lordships** (`Docs/Territories.md` is the brief of record, four
passes): `CastleSite` is a scene component on the georeferenced marker
objects — Grosmont = player home (the user's call), Skenfrith and White
Castle the neighbors (White Castle marker + mat added this session; the
Marches map verified linear in Web Mercator, kx=kz to 7 decimals).
Sites are scene facts referenced by index into the name-sorted list;
`TerritoryOf` (nearest site) partitions the whole map; a siteless scene
(Spiš) synthesizes one home site. Parcels exist only within
`SurveyReach` of their own castle; `MaxSurveyRadius` and the
center-distance ownership rings are DELETED. Classification went
RELATIVE per territory (flattest ~35% farm → ~70% pasture → timber +
Perlin forest mask) after absolute slope thresholds dealt the hill map
219 timber, 1 farm, no village. Ownership follows the lordship: your
demesne settled, the rest of your territory bandit-held (the land
ladder's first rung), neighbor territories wholly theirs. `LandLaw`:
the player builds masonry only on their own Castle parcels — wired into
the live blocked flags and `SlabPolyRefusal` so red ghost and commit
gate are one test; stands down when no player Castle parcel exists so
old saves keep building. Survey tool: clearing bandit land is
adjacency-gated (one-test frontier rule), buying a neighbor's parcel is
negotiation at 6 years' purchase (1.5 for bandit land) with no
adjacency — kilometres of gap would forbid it forever. CastleSave v7:
each plot carries its territory; v6 plots migrate once by nearest site.

**2. The survey dials + feature-snapped boundaries**: `LandSurvey`
("Land Survey Settings" object) — cellSize, surveyReach, settled-plot
counts; Resurvey context menu re-runs in play mode. surveyReach 0 =
survey the whole map, lordships butting at the seams (verified: 5,625
plots at the user's chosen 200m cells); a 25,000-plot guard refuses
GameObject soup. `SnapCornersToFeatures`: a lattice corner within 0.38
cells of a road/river polyline snaps onto it (accepted only within 0.45
cells of its grid seat, so jitter+snap can't cross the quad) —
boundaries follow the features, the user's ask. `LandFeatures` is the
ONE runtime door to the Marches feature polylines.

**3. The roads of 1220** (the user's call: "keep the current road
system, but only keep the roads that connect castles"): `CastleRoads`
stitches the 1,872 OSM ways into a vertex graph (2.5m merge) and keeps
the pairwise Dijkstra routes between castle sites — 3 routes.
`MarchesDressing.castleRoadsOnly` (default on) filters the Dress bake;
splats, hedgerows and tree keep-outs all read the filtered set, and the
runtime planter recomputes the same routes deterministically so paint
and tree gaps agree by construction.

**4. Timber grows oaks** (`TimberWoods.Plant`): deterministic planting
inside Timber parcels — skipping real woodland, castle routes and
riverbanks — through `TerrainPads.SessionData` only (the session-clone
door), replants truncating to each tile's dressing-time instance count
so they never stack. `timberTreeSpacing` dial. Frame rate held.

**5. The castles round** (the user's four asks in one directive):

- **Castle grounds are a terrain-fitted polygon** (`CastleGrounds`), not
  a survey cell: 36 radial rays from the site stop where ground leaves
  ±6m of the site's own ground, clamped 60–140m, two smoothing passes —
  dials on LandSurvey (`castlePlots` deleted; castle size no longer
  couples to cell size). Measured: Grosmont 31,082m², Skenfrith
  61,263m², White Castle 51,238m² — genuinely terrain-different.
  Lattice cells inside stand aside; nearby corners snap to the boundary
  as a feature line. `CastleGrounds` reads dials via
  `FindAnyObjectByType` fallback because the edit-mode Dress bake never
  sees `LandSurvey.Instance` — bake and runtime must read the same
  numbers.
- **The road ends at the gate**: `CastleRoads.TrimIntoGrounds` cuts each
  route end back to the LAST point inside its destination's polygon,
  bisecting to the boundary crossing — the trim point IS the gate. Full
  Dress rebake repainted the trimmed routes (verified: no paint inside
  any bailey; "438 water ribbons, 214,527 trees/bushes").
- **The village lies on the road before the gate**: `SettleTerritory`
  claims the cells each trimmed approach passes through, walking out
  from the gate, round-robin across approaches (all six Living plots
  verified 7–64m from their road); fields and pasture still grow
  flattest-adjacent around the settled core; Spiš falls back to
  flatness.
- **Neighbor castles are SCENERY** (the user chose "prefabs from real
  plans" over teaching the computer to build): graybox massings under a
  "Neighbor Castles" root — Skenfrith's quadrilateral curtain, corner
  drums and central round keep; White Castle's pear-shaped ward and
  twin-towered gatehouse — primitives with colliders, outside
  `splineParent`, in no registry and no save: untouchable by player
  tools by construction. The marker cubes' renderers went off at both
  sites (Grosmont's stays — the player builds the home castle).

**6. Docs**: `Docs/Territories.md` written (four passes), the CLAUDE.md
survey section rewritten to match, dressing bullet updated.

## Decisions & trade-offs

- **Lordships, not rings**: the deep country between territories is
  claimed, unworked, lawless and UNPARCELED — bandit camps at the seams
  wait for combat; no seam machinery before its consumer.
- **River-weighted territory seams deferred** — the user: "lets do just
  number 1 for now" (timber trees) when offered both.
- **Neighbor castles as scenery** over real wall-graph builds: real
  walls need an edge OWNER field the graph doesn't have; the graybox is
  honest about that instead of faking it.
- **Boundary cells may overlap the castle polygon slightly** (slivers at
  survey scale) — accepted; the cell-mid test is the cheap one.
- **A route passing near a THIRD castle mid-run is not trimmed** —
  deliberate; only ends are gates.
- Income is ~11× hot at 200m cells — known, waits for the cell size to
  settle before rebalancing.

## Bugs hit

- **Absolute classification thresholds** on the hill map: 219 timber,
  1 farm, no village → relative-per-territory quantiles.
- **The village scattered** across the territory → one contiguous town
  growing by grid adjacency from the castle's own parcel, then (pass
  four) seeded from the road approaches instead.
- **The road clipped THROUGH Grosmont's grounds** after the first trim
  (leading-run strip): the nearest road vertex sits BEYOND the polygon,
  so the end looked already-outside and the clip survived — 4/519
  points inside. `TrimIntoGrounds` scans for the LAST inside point from
  each end; re-verified 0/489, all six route ends 2.3–10.8m from
  boundary corners.
- **CS0103 across four files** after adding CastleGrounds.cs — the new
  file hadn't been imported; `refresh_unity` force-mode fixed it.
- **Mid-play recompile husked the plots** once (a known hazard) — stop
  play before editing scripts.
- The MCP bridge dropped during the multi-minute Dress rebake (known);
  completion verified via an Editor.log watcher, not a re-run.
- `GetMethod("ClearDressing")` hit AmbiguousMatchException (two
  overloads) — both methods are public, called directly.
- **Unity crashed overnight** after the final save. At wrap the
  recovery scene (`Assets/_Recovery/0.unity`) was diffed against
  `Marches.unity` with object IDs normalized: zero differing lines,
  identical object inventories — nothing newer existed; the folder was
  deleted. The empty 3.5KB second recovery file was a blank untitled
  scene.
