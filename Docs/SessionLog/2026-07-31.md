## What we did — day 1 (2026-07-30)

- **`WallRoom` (new)**: a designated room = a stored directed ring of graph edges (`WallGraph.DirEdge`), outline sampled once at designation. Floor slab (top = ceil of max ground, so terrain never pokes through); roof deck **flush** with the wall tops, footprint inset to the walls' inner faces (mitered `InsetOutline`), ear-clip triangulated. Roof requires all boundary stack-tops level within 0.05 (`StackTopOver` climbs course stacks); failures return a reason string to the build log, and `NotifyCourseLaid` retries roofless rooms as courses level them. `NotifySplit` splices sub-edges into rings so splits never orphan a room; **breach dissolves the whole room** — floor, roof, record (user's rule: floors follow the roof's auto-delete).
- **`WallGraph.TraceFace`**: keep-left face walk (min clockwise angle from the arrival's inward bearing) — CCW interior rings with positive shoelace, first try. `Connected` BFS powers the room chain's closure test. `CoincidentCoverage` measures how much of a proposed edge lies along existing masonry.
- **Room tool**: chained wall placement that auto-stops on enclosure — each click a vertex, segments clamp at the first crossing, the chain commits atomically when the end reaches masonry connected to the start, then the enclosed face designates. Chain winding picks the trace side.
- **Shared shape helpers** (user chose over per-tool objects): Circle (8 quadratic arcs, ~0.3% error) and Rect for both Walls and Room tools; sides fully coincident with existing walls are **reused** (the wall becomes the side), partial overlaps refuse.
- **Menu rework**: Curved/Offset became left-panel checkboxes; "Wall Segment" → "Palisade"; bar = Walls | Room | Delete. **`BuildLog` (new)**: collapsible top-left panel explaining outcomes ("Room designated — 5 walls, roofed." / why not).
- **`Docs/Roadmap.md` (new)**: the vision confirmed feasible (player-designed walkable castle, purposeful rooms, sieges where design matters), five phases with gates, prior-art anchors, procedural detailing added to the potential-feature list.
- **Playtest round 1**: roof sat proud by its thickness → flush inset deck (coplanar-culling reasoning: opposite-facing coplanar is safe, same-facing z-fights). Rect had no snapping → anchor/corner snaps + side reuse. No failure feedback → BuildLog.
- **Playtest round 2**: breach rule (above); snapped-start hovers borrow the wall tool's presentation instead of burying a ghost box in the host; shape gestures adopt snapped height (`shapeSnapHeight`); and **elevated ground** — `WallEdge.baseY` plumbed through params/splits/deletes, room slabs are build surfaces (`TryGetBuildSurface`), rooms on decks skip their floor (the deck IS the floor) — second and third storeys emerged from one field.

## What we did — day 2 (2026-07-31)

- **Storey adoption** (user: heights "revert" when starting from an elevated wall — empirically diagnosed as *elevation*, not height): snaps now adopt the host's `baseY` (`AdoptSnapElevation`), so gestures started on a second-storey wall build beside it, not on the terrain below. Snapping works ON decks too, filtered to same-deck walls (`BaseMatches`) — the masonry a storey down never grabs the gesture. Offset tool carries its source's storey.
- **"Two overlapping end nodes"** — answered: one node per turn is the rule AND the fact; the gold node highlight was shelling both member end sections (which genuinely overlap in the corner square) with the grey post unlit between them. The post now glows too, and all shell ghosts (`FitShell`) inflate about the **mesh's own center** — the old transform-pivot scaling floated world-space meshes (the red delete overlay hovering above the roof was the same bug: slab coordinates × 1.02).
- **Angle readout + Shift stepping** (user: wrong degrees at corners; steps should land on 5s): the arc was measuring against `MostOpenDir`'s reverse — an abstract bisector. `AngleRefBearing` now measures against the real wall run forming the tightest corner with the drag; Shift steps **relative to that same reference** (`StepBearingFrom`) — clean multiples even off the world grid. The room chain gained the arc (corner vs previous segment; 180 = straight on).
- **Delete rework** (user diagnosed the drag dying at the first node; chose both halves): press ON a wall **paints through nodes** — the doomed path re-derives every frame from anchor to cursor (`EdgePath` BFS; partial spans on first/last edges, whole edges between; backing up shrinks); press on empty ground **rubber-bands a marquee** (screen-space red band, sections by projected centers, rooms un-room via slab centers). `WallGraph.DeleteSections` gained a **set-based overload** — an edge can only be surgered once (survivors are new edges), so all its doomed indices go in one call. Escape cancels either gesture.
- **Tower symmetry** (four-tower curtain build): diagnosed the "uneven by 4.5m with lock-top on" roof failure — lock-top planes are **per gesture**; only a snapped *start* adopts the host's plane, and the ring includes reused curtain spans that must be level too. The failure log now names the culprit (`CompassFrom` octant: "lowest on the southwest side"). Then the **rotated 3-click rect** (user chose rect-first over the room stamp): corner → side at any bearing (Shift = 5° relative, snapped far end) → width (Shift = square), with **live dimension labels** (side/width/radius) — identical towers by reading numbers, flush along rotated walls.
- **Corner-tower snap** (user: tower whose two face-centers meet two curtain-wall ends): **Ctrl+click = side-midpoint anchor**, auto-perpendicular on a free wall end; the **length solves** when the second wall's end projects onto the side line, the **width solves** when the adjacent side's midpoint catches it. Verified exact (6.0/8.0 on off-grid walls) — and the commit fuses for real: the side chain-splits through the curtain's end node, valence 3 at both junctions.
- **White alignment guides**: extension lines whenever a placed point sits on a straight wall's line extension (all gestures — wall drag, chain tip, rect ends/corners), suppressed on occupied spans, collinear split-pieces deduped to one line; plus solve-tie lines when the corner-tower snaps fire.
- **The shape lock-plane bug** (user's two rooms-on-a-room's-wall failed at exactly 0.5 off, live scene inspected): the figure's locked plane was recomputed per preview frame from `EffectiveWallHeight` — by then the hover snap was gone, so the HUD height leaked in. The plane is now **captured at the anchor click** (`shapeTopY`), the mechanism the room chain already used. Verified worst-case: HUD 8.75 vs host plane 19 → committed flat at 19, roofed.

## Decisions & trade-offs

- **Rooms are stored designations, not inferred spaces** — the ring is data; splits splice it, breaches dissolve it. No re-derivation, per graph doctrine.
- **Roof-or-nothing honesty**: uneven tops leave a room floor-only with a named reason rather than a guessed roof. Leveling (courses, lock-top) is the player's move; the log points at the low side.
- **Breach kills the whole room** (user's rule) — no half-alive rooms; walls always survive.
- **Slabs are ground** — one `baseY` float made storeys emergent; the planar graph stays height-blind (upper walls crossing lower walls in XZ still split them) — deliberately deferred to the ⚠ storeys/stairs design session.
- **Lock-top planes stay per-gesture**; snapped starts adopt exactly. Cross-gesture plane management is workflow (chain from corners), not new machinery.
- **Delete gestures split by press target** (wall = paint, ground = marquee) — same convention as RTS/editor select; each mode covers the other's weakness.
- **Marquee selects by screen projection, centers-in-rect** — occluded walls in the box DO die; the red shells are the confirmation surface.
- **Shift is phase-contextual in the rect** (5° direction steps laying the side, square constraint pulling width) — one modifier, the meaning the phase needs.
- **Room stamp deferred** (user: "both — rect first") — backlogged for the openings/storeys era.

## Bugs hit

- **CS0165/CS0136/CS0103 round** (day 1): out-var in short-circuit, `count` collision, new-file force-refresh.
- **Preview-key −∞ swallowing** (twice, day 1): tuple keys collapsing when top unlocked — widened the tuples.
- **`EdgeParams` struct-default hazard** (self-caught): object initializers omitting `baseY` get 0, not −∞ — every initializer must set it explicitly.
- **World-space-mesh shell scaling** (day 2): ghost shells scaled about the transform pivot drift by 2% of the distance from the pivot — invisible on wall sections (local pivots), a 0.4m float on room slabs. `FitShell` scales about the mesh bounds center everywhere.
- **`EffectiveWallHeight` amnesia** (day 2, the room-on-room failure): any code reading it outside the hover/click frame gets the HUD height, not the adopted snap — the shape plane and anything like it must capture on the click frame.
- Console noise catalogued: "PlayerLoop called recursively" = MCP screenshot capture during play (memory note saved); "referenced script missing" spam = `Assets/_Recovery` + domain-reload artifacts, not compile failures.
