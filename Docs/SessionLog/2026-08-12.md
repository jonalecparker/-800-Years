# Session — 2026-08-11/12: the terrain becomes tiles, roads become paint, rivers become real

*One conversation (several compactions) spanning the 11th evening into
the 12th, all uncommitted until now. The user asked for roads and rivers
done "the right way once": that meant the systemic fix first — the
single 15km terrain became a 4×4 tile grid behind a `Ground` facade —
then roads painted INTO the ground (and their ribbons deleted), then the
rivers pass: carved beds, two-tier water (cheap ribbons everywhere, HDRP
water near the camera — the user's own distance-swap idea), riverbed
paint, brook-scale waves. Three rounds of the user's screenshots drove
three real fixes.*

## Goal

Make roads and rivers realistic without patching: roads as a terrain
type instead of a mesh on top; rivers carved into the terrain with
water in them, cheap far / expensive near.

## What we did

**1. The tile grid + the Ground facade** (`Docs/TerrainTiles.md` is the
brief): one Unity terrain caps at 4096 alphamap ≈ 3.7m/px over 15km —
too coarse for a 3m lane, and un-fixable without splitting. So:

- `TerrainTiler` (editor bake on the source terrain): 4×4 tiles of
  3771m, 513² heightmaps that SHARE edge samples by construction (seams
  cannot open), 1024² alphamaps (3.68m/px — 8×), LODs stitched by
  auto-connect group 800. The source terrain stays in the scene,
  deactivated: it is the re-bake authority. Bake order is Generate →
  Split → Carve → Dress; re-running Split is the un-carve.
- `Ground` (new static facade) is now the ONLY way scripts read terrain:
  `HeightAt`, `TileAt`, `Bounds`, `BaseName` (strips " [r,c]" so old
  saves keep matching). Migrated: `WallEdge`, `WalkMode`,
  `GridPlacementSystem`, `LandParcels`, `LandPlot`, `CastleSave`.
  Cursor raycasts stay type-based (`hit.collider is TerrainCollider`)
  and needed no change.
- `TerrainPads` rewritten per-tile (pristine cache, preview snapshots,
  alphamap refresh all per tile; ops stay world-space so the save
  format did not move). The deviation law, KeepOut, replay — all
  verified intact across seams.
- Full battery: heights vs source 0.0001m; seam mismatch 0.0000m;
  bench across a seam continuous; law clamps at pristine−3 exactly;
  preview cancel bit-perfect; 16/16 quadrant raycasts; 48/48 LOD
  neighbor links; Spiš single-terrain regression clean.

**2. Roads became splat paint, and ONLY paint** (tier B's first slice,
then the user's call "just the terrain" retired the ribbons entirely):
`RoadLayer` (the dirt layer's real textures, warm remap — a flat white
diffuse reads alpha=1 as FULL smoothness in HDRP TerrainLit and shone
like wet sand) painted along all 1,872 corridors, full over roadbed+1m,
fading 2.5m. The road ribbon group and its assets are deleted;
`TerrainPads.RefreshSurface` preserves layers past grass/dirt so
earthworks keep the paint (verified: bench a path across a lane, road
weight 1.000 before and after; ResetToPristine restores).

**3. Rivers** (`Docs/Rivers.md` is the brief of record):

- **Carve** (`CarveRivers`, edit-mode, idempotent): every waterway cut
  into the tiles — depth clamp(width×0.22, 0.4–1.8m), Monnow measures
  1.72–1.86m below bank, streams 0.2–0.4 (the 7.37m cells' honest
  limit). 124,457 stamps, 89.5k samples lowered, seams bit-perfect
  across all 24 tile pairs, re-run a measured no-op. TerrainPads'
  pristine picks up the carved bed, so the ±3m law measures from it —
  rivers can be bridged, never filled.
- **Two-tier water**: ribbons flat at the water line, edges buried in
  the banks, chunked by 300m CELL (1,332 chunks, `RiverChunk` bake
  facts: level range + max width). `RiverWaterLOD` (new, ticks by
  design like tree LOD) swaps the nearest ≤4 chunks within 260m to
  HDRP `WaterSurface` custom-mesh instances and back — verified both
  directions. Read the HDRP source: custom-mesh water KEEPS vertex
  heights (waterToWorld = identity) and draws the mesh explicitly, so
  sloping reaches render sloped and the steep-reach guard (8m/chunk)
  is cosmetic only. ~7ms for 3 sims in-editor. `supportWater` flipped
  on in the shared HDRP Performant asset (harmless to Spiš).
- **User round 1** (looks good; bed is grass; waves too big):
  `RiverbedLayer` (4th splat layer, dark wet mud, river BEATS road
  where they meet — a ford is mud) painted under every channel; HDRP's
  river wave defaults are SEA-sized (largeWindSpeed 30) → Waves dials
  at brook scale, scaled per chunk by `RiverChunk.maxWidth` (Monnow
  largeWind 3.0, streams 0.9).
- **User round 2** (hillside screenshot: black zigzag gashes; ribbons
  pop in close): cross-slope over-dig — bank refs 11m out took the
  LOWER side, metres downhill on a slope. `ChannelRef` now samples
  CLOSE-IN (centerline + just past the channel edge) → terrace notch.
  And water moved from bed+fill to **ref − freeboard (waterFill 0.7)**,
  floored above the bed — near grade it survives the terrain LOD.
- **User round 3** (still dashed far away): the terrain LOD's height
  error is SCREEN-space — it GROWS with distance, so no fixed raise
  wins. `RiverWaterLOD` now lifts far chunks 0.004/m past 250m (cap
  8m) — sub-pixel at the range it applies, zeroed near and on upgrade,
  never baked into meshes. Long-valley shot: continuous water to the
  horizon.
- Trees keep out of the water (same cell mechanism as roads): 212,951
  instances, ~36k riverside trunks gone.

## Decisions & trade-offs

- **Tile now, stream later**: the grid + facade are the permanent
  Britain-scale foundation; streaming/floating origin are additive on a
  tile grid and wait until we leave 15km (float precision ~2mm here).
- **Splats stay 1024**: tile assets held at 103MB total; 2048 would
  quadruple them. `splatResolution` is the dial for corridors that earn
  it.
- **The LOD seam visible from altitude** (HDRP water ↔ ribbon) is a
  KNOWN deferred item — revisit with performance budget (candidates in
  the brief).
- Deferred with reasons in the brief: flow-direction currents, foam,
  waterfalls at chunk steps, water gameplay, per-corridor heightmap
  upscaling.

## Bugs hit

- **The carve RATCHETED** (caught by the second-run test): bank refs
  sampled from live tiles sink at confluences/meanders where one
  channel's carve reaches another's bank point — every re-run dug
  deeper. Refs now come from the immutable pre-split source; re-run
  converged to 30 quantum stragglers and 0.00000m of movement.
- **16-bit heightmap quantum**: SetHeights rounds to 1/65535; half of
  all writes round UP, so a bare `<` re-fired forever (44k phantom
  "moves" per run). One-quantum epsilon.
- **Cross-slope over-dig** and **screen-space LOD error** — the two
  user-screenshot fixes above; both are now doctrine in the brief.
- HDRP smoothness-in-diffuse-alpha (roads shone like wet sand);
  `diffuseRemapMax` is a Vector4 (`.w`, not `.a`).
- Tooling: execute_code can't declare classes (reflection on the
  private `Fea` types instead); reflection GetField needs `Public` for
  public fields of a private class; the MCP bridge WebSocket drops
  during multi-minute bakes and reconnects (state-check, don't re-run —
  the "Marches dressed" log line is identical every bake).
