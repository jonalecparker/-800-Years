# Session — 2026-08-14/15: the BNG world frame, tiles as artifacts, and the land dressed to explain itself

*One conversation (several compactions) spanning the 14th into the
15th. Two arcs: the castle-tile resolution question became a full
world-frame rebuild — the game's world is now the British National
Grid, tiles bake deterministically from source rasters, and the old
Mercator frame, source terrain and LIDAR patcher are deleted — and
the first flyovers over the new 2m ground drove a dressing arc
(hedgebanks, field mottle, thalweg-snapped rivers, river-resolution
tiles) plus build-tool improvements (foundation shapes, chain angle
stepping).*

## Goal

Decide the castle-tile resolution bump (the 08-14 wrap's chosen
topic). The user pushed it to first principles — hundreds of castles
across England, streaming planned before anything is built that
streaming would replace — and approved `Docs/WorldFrame.md`.

## What we did

**1. The world frame** (`WorldFrame.cs`, `Docs/WorldFrame.md`): world
XZ = BNG easting/northing − (332000, 210000), world Y = metres ODN.
One static class owns every conversion (WGS84↔BNG via Helmert + Airy
TM, the grid-ref namer, the legacy Mercator chain for migration only).
Verified off-editor to millimetre round-trips and the castles' real
grid references. The Marches window is E332–348/N210–226 — shifted
2km southwest from the draft after mapping actual ASC coverage.

**2. The tile bake** (`MarchesBaker.cs` — THE TILE IS THE ARTIFACT):
8×8 2km tiles on even-km BNG lines, named by grid ref ("Marches
[SO4024]"), each baked independently from the NRW 2m LIDAR (flight
strips MERGED per sample — the old per-cell dictionary kept one file
arbitrarily; the west column is 40–98% NODATA per strip) with OS
Terrain 50 as the full-GB fallback (both BNG+ODN native — no
reprojection, no datum fitting; the terrarium DEM and per-site offset
fitting are gone). The 2m LIDAR IS the base DEM — the separate
patch/feather step dissolved. Rivers carve inside the bake from
pre-carve in-memory refs, SAVED to `MarchesRiverRefs.json` so the
ribbons never re-derive from carved ground. `TerrainTiler.cs` and
`CastleLidar.cs` deleted, not dormant; un-carve = re-bake.
One-click scene migration moved sites and the neighbor-castle graybox
through the legacy chain (with the ~0.7° grid-convergence yaw) and
deleted the old world. `CastleSave` v8 migrates pre-v8 Marches saves
once (XZ through the legacy chain, absolute Y + 23.85m); Spiš saves
never migrate. Bake verified in-editor: 64 tiles in 139s, 9 castle
tiles at 1025 (Grosmont's 4th expected tile correctly missed the 600m
rule by 98m), 0.03% unfillable confined to the far-NE LIDAR gap.

**3. The features re-bake** (`Tools/bake_marches_features.py`,
committed this time): Overpass → BNG frame → DP 1.5m → clip to
window+200m. First hand-test caught **the castle routes broken** —
DP had deleted mid-way JUNCTION vertices (OSM ways that join share
the literal node), shattering the road graph into 800 components;
Skenfrith's nearest vertex was a 2-vertex orphan that's genuinely
disconnected in OSM. Fixed twice: the bake force-keeps shared
coordinates through DP (roads only — waters/woods stayed
byte-identical so the river refs kept aligning), and `CastleRoads`
snaps castles to the nearest vertex ON THE LARGEST COMPONENT.
Verified offline: 40 components, all three castle pairs route.
`LandParcels.SurveySignature` now mixes the features asset — the
floods stripe against roads/rivers, so a re-baked features file
invalidates the cache by itself.

**4. The dressing arc** (all from the user's flyover judgments):

- *The naked-earthworks read*: the LIDAR keeps field-boundary banks
  but strips the hedges that explain them. `MarchesDressing` grew a
  HEDGEBANK PASS — crest detection on the fine tiles (proud of a 7m
  ring by `hedgeBankSignal`; ring spread >4m = hillside, skip;
  keep-outs: road/water cells, woods, castle grounds) — and FIELD
  MOTTLE (two-octave world-anchored noise leaning the grass/dirt mix,
  `splatMottle`). First run planted 120k hedges but ALL in the
  southern tiles: the cap was first-come in tile order, and the
  global 300k tree cap then quietly thinned the woods to make room.
  Fixed: collect-then-thin (fair hash spread map-wide), tree budget
  raised to 450k (script AND scene component — the stairWidth trap).
  Final: 119,737 hedges from 221,066 candidates, 395k total
  instances, no global thinning.
- *The sideways double-carve*: OSM centerlines are generalized; the
  real channel meanders ±5–10m around them, and the carve cut a
  braided second trench where they diverged. The carve now
  THALWEG-SNAPS — each station scans the corridor for the lowest
  existing ground, snaps only when it sits ≥0.3m below its own banks,
  smoothed along the run; snapped stations are SAVED in the refs and
  the water ribbons AND bed splat follow the channel as-built.
- *Angular rivers on coarse tiles*: resolution now follows FEATURES —
  tiles crossed by a waterway ≥5m wide bake at 513 (23 tiles). Edge
  pinning generalized to three tiers (coarsest pins first) plus a
  corner-consensus pass (four-tile lattice corners take the coarsest
  tile's value — pin order alone could split them by cm).

**5. Build-tool improvements** (the user's asks, end of session):
the Circle/Rectangle CHECKBOXES now arm foundation shapes exactly as
they arm wall figures (an R-cycle shipped first; the user preferred
the existing checkboxes and R was removed same session) — rect is
3 clicks (corner → first side → width; Shift steps the side's angle /
squares the width), circle is centre → radius as a 24-gon; both use
the chain's plane machinery, refusal and commit door
(`CommitSlabPoly`, factored). The outline chain (foundations AND
floors) gained the corner-angle arc, leg length guides, and Shift
5° stepping — snaps and the closing pull outrank it, per doctrine.

## Decisions & trade-offs

- **World = BNG, streaming-first**: every terrain number was an
  inherited accident (`terrainSize=(res-1)*demCellSize`); now tiles,
  data products (NRW 2km / EA 1km / OS 10km) and identity all sit on
  the same even-km lattice, and nothing built today needs changing
  when streaming lands (Phase B/C in the brief).
- **Resolution follows features, not just castles** — the tile ladder
  (257/513/1025) is the mechanism; castles and rivers are just rules.
- **Hedges yield to woods, capped and fair** — dressing dials
  (`hedgeBankSignal`, `hedgeBushStep`, `splatMottle`,
  `maxTreeInstances` 450k) are the user's to tune by eye.
- Unity's "different heightmap resolution — stop neighboring" spam at
  mixed-res seams is accepted (geometry is crack-free by pinning;
  auto-connect just can't stitch LOD across resolutions).

## Bugs hit

- The junction-vertex loss and the orphan-stub snap (above) — the
  console's "castles share no road route" was the tell; reproduced
  offline with a Python re-implementation of the graph stitch.
- The hedge cap spending itself before Grosmont's row (above) —
  found by counting bushes per tile (2,131 on a castle tile that
  detection said deserved ~10k).
- A stale MCP session silently eating every execute_code call
  (success:false, null message) — a fresh `initialize` fixed it;
  long main-thread bakes must be fired via
  `EditorApplication.delayCall` and verified from console STATE.
- The console flooded by the auto-connect spam faster than polls
  could read it — `read_console` has a `filter_text` param; use it.
- The wall-to-foundation rim snap failed for the user on their FIRST
  play (before walk mode; second play fine every time) — NOT
  reproduced: live probes of the full resolver (before/after
  simulated walk round trips and a real StandDown) all pass, no
  stranded modifier keys. Left open with a watch plan.
