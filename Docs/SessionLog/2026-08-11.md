# Session — 2026-08-10/11: the estate earns, the world goes real, and the Marches get dressed

*One long conversation (several compactions) in three arcs, all
uncommitted until now: the income side of the economy (survey, calendar,
quarter-day revenue, CastleSave v6); the open-world direction settled —
real Britain, real castle sites — and proven with a 15km Monmouthshire
scene holding Grosmont and Skenfrith; then the realism pass that dressed
it with real roads, woods and rivers from OpenStreetMap so the
castle-to-castle walk is worth taking. Two small hand-test fixes opened
the session (rim snap from terrain, foundation pricing).*

## Goal

Build the income side of the economy (land types, ownership, the four
asks), then follow where the design conversation led: how big is the
world, is it real terrain, and what does the ride between two castles
feel like.

## What we did

**1. Hand-test fixes**: walls stopped snapping to foundation rims because
a near-grade pad exposes no skirt for the ray to catch — a TERRAIN hit
within `RimAdoptBand` (MaxSkirt+1m) now adopts the slab
(`RimSlabFromTerrain`, both resolvers, Alt opts out). Foundations
out-pricing their palisades: `FoundationRate` 18 → 4d/m³.

**2. The survey, the calendar and income** (design decisions by the
user: irregular parcels, pay-to-clear bandits, real-time slow clock with
a speed slider):

- `LandPlot` — stored polygon with a nature (`Farm/Pasture/Timber/
  Living`), an owner (`Player/Bandits/Neighbor`) and survey-grid coords;
  generated ONCE by `LandParcels.EnsureGenerated` (jittered 60m lattice),
  facts forever after. Classification is RELATIVE (relief quantiles +
  Perlin forest mask); the demesne is SETTLED land (flattest parcels
  become village/fields/pasture whatever the wild grade says). Overlay
  drapes current ground each showing; collider enabled only in the
  Survey tool.
- **The Survey tool** (`ToolMode.Land`): hover reads a parcel into the
  InspectLine, click clears bandit land / buys a neighbor's through
  `Estate.Pay`. The frontier rule is one-test (`TouchesPlayerLand`
  grid adjacency). Prices from yield: 6 years' purchase peaceful, 1.5
  bandit-held.
- **`GameClock`** — 60 real s/game day at 1×, HUD slider exponential to
  120×, 365-day calendar from Lady Day 1220. `DaysElapsed` is the one
  stored fact; ticks in `BuildMenu.Update` and deliberately does NOT
  stand down. Income lands on the four quarter days
  (`Estate.CollectQuarter`, Michaelmas ×1.6, lean ×0.8): produce by
  parcel area + household dues (12 households to start). Measured:
  ~£18/yr for the starting demesne — top of the knight band.
- **CastleSave v6**: plots, `clockDays`, `households`. Pre-v6 saves load
  with no parcels (survey regenerates next frame), Lady Day 1220,
  twelve households.

**3. The world direction** (decided in conversation, recorded in
memory + `Docs/MarchesRealism.md`): **real Britain (England+Wales),
Europe-ready architecture** — the deciding factors were that army march
time is decoupled from map size (armies march on the compressed
calendar) and the user definitely wants real castle locations (~1000
recorded sites). Terrain is tiered: 30m satellite macro, 1m national
LIDAR at castle sites later, synthesis between. Design verdicts on the
income slice: **one manor = one contiguous town by the castle** (the
scattered living plots are wrong), and **bandit land belongs at the
seams BETWEEN lordships** — castle sites → territories → land types,
which needs the castle-sites layer to exist first.

**4. The Marches scene** (`Assets/Marches.unity`): 25 terrarium z13
tiles mosaicked → `MarchesDem.bytes` (2049² float32, 7.37m cells, 15km
of real Monmouthshire) → `MarchesTerrain.asset` through the existing
`TerrainGenerator` DEM path. Grosmont (red) and Skenfrith (blue) marker
cubes 6.6km apart, georeferencing verified against Graig Syfyrddin's
real summit to one pixel. Spiš confirmed 1m (user question; the old
scene is untouched). The user flew it: **the distance feels right.**

**5. The Marches dressing** (`MarchesDressing.cs`, brief in
`Docs/MarchesRealism.md` — the "how far can realism reasonably go"
exploration, tiered A–E, with A+D shipped same-day):

- Overpass/OSM for the exact bbox: 2,152 roads, 569 woods, 511
  waterways — preprocessed OFFLINE (python, Douglas-Peucker, the
  verified georeferencing) into `Assets/Terrain/MarchesFeatures.json`.
  Unity never fetches.
- **Roads are draped ribbon meshes** (the 29.5m/px alphamap can't hold
  a 3m track): through-roads 4m, lanes 3m, paths 1.8m, ALL the same
  dirt (the 1220 filter — rural lanes follow their medieval routes).
  **Water ribbons**: the Monnow 8m, streams 2m. 1,872 + 438 ribbons in
  9 chunk meshes, saved as assets. No colliders (the walker must never
  trip on a road).
- **249,019 terrain trees/bushes**: low-poly oak (32 tris, trunk
  collider) and bush (20 tris) prefabs built in code, scattered
  deterministically (hashed, never Random) inside wood polygons; roads
  through woods stay clear (spatial-hash road cells); bushes also hedge
  the lanes. One road runs right past the Grosmont marker.
- **Tier D atmosphere**: `MarchesSkyFog.asset` — a CLONE of the shared
  Sky and Fog profile (the Spiš scene keeps its own) — volumetric fog,
  volumetric clouds, warm afternoon sun.
- ~50fps in editor play mode with the survey's 1,957 parcels on top.

## Decisions & trade-offs

- **Money and time**: construction stays instant, deletion refunds in
  full, treasury can go into debt — all carry over; income arrives only
  on quarter days (a load never back-pays skipped quarters).
- **`MaxSurveyRadius` 1500m is a TEMPORARY guard** — an unbounded survey
  on 15km is 62k parcels. Castle territories replace it.
- **Ribbons over alphamap paint, terrain trees over scattered
  GameObjects, offline bake over live fetch** — each recorded with
  reasoning in the brief.
- Deferred deliberately: houses on living land, manor decision dials,
  hedgerows-from-parcels (tier B), Welsh 1m LIDAR at the sites (tier C),
  trunk roads dropped rather than reinterpreted, wood multipolygon
  relations (closed ways only).

## Bugs hit

- **LandPlot overlay invisible AND unhoverable**: `SlabTile.Triangulate`
  on CCW verts faces DOWN — raycasts ignore backfaces. Flip after
  triangulating.
- **The entire road network invisible** — two independent causes,
  found the hard way: (1) a draped mesh fights the terrain's rendered
  LOD, not its heightmap — `heightmapPixelError` lets the drawn ground
  deviate both ways and swallow a 0.12m lift wholesale (now pixelError
  2 + 0.30m lift); (2) the ribbon winding was MIRRORED — the debug
  probe quad "reproducing" the builder had L/R swapped and rendered
  fine while every real chunk was backface-culled. Doctrine: read the
  baked normals back before trusting a probe reproduction.
- **Trees culled at ~50m** (user's hand-test): `treeDistance` isn't the
  only dial — `treeBillboardDistance` defaults to 50m and
  `treeMaximumFullLODCount` to 50, and mesh trees have no billboard to
  swap to, so they vanished. All dials now 2500m/200k.
- **The serialized-field trap, twice**: `roadLift` script-default
  change did nothing (scene value wins) — the `stairWidth` lesson
  re-learned.
- Primitive-sphere trees were ~760 tris (unshippable at 249k
  instances) → hand-built icosahedron/prism meshes.
- Absolute slope thresholds classified the hill map 219 timber / 1
  farm / 0 village → relative quantiles + curated settled demesne.
- Same-call collider-sync gotcha again (survey hover test); Overpass
  rate limit hit once (spaced the queries); Skenfrith reads ~30m low
  (satellite-composite wobble, shape correct — footnote, not fixed).

## Verification status

**Machine-verified**: income transactions to the penny (buy, clear,
quarter payday exactly on the day crossing); v6 snapshot round-trip
(owners, clock, households); pre-v6 load regenerates the survey;
georeferencing to one pixel of the real summit; ribbon normals up;
road-at-the-gate, woods, river and tree draw distance all confirmed in
scripted HDRP renders; play mode boots clean at ~50fps.

**Hand-tested by the user**: the survey overlay (which produced the
one-town-per-manor and bandits-at-seams verdicts), the Grosmont →
Skenfrith flight, and the 50m tree-cull catch. **Not yet**: the actual
walk, the Survey purchases in anger, volumetric clouds (temporal —
scripted renders can't converge them), the clock slider feel.
