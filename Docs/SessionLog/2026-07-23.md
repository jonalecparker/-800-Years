## Decisions & trade-offs

- **Realistic over stylized:** costs more (characters, performance, no style camouflage) but is the vision-aligned call — "the period is the star." Kingdom Come as reference point.
- **Modular placement over freeform drawing:** keeps creative/defensive castle-building while dropping the two worst engineering problems; freeform judged a scope trap (a second full game).
- **Pipeline over inventory:** no mass art production before gameplay; only the Phase 1 set + a repeatable process. ~40% design drift expected.
- **Gate lifted deliberately**, not drifted past — with the explicit caveat that game one's momentum needs protecting.
