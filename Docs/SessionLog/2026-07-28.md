# Session — 2026-07-27 → 2026-07-28

## Goal

Make connected layouts — squares, corners, tie-ins — easy to build. The user opened with a four-item spec: exact snapping to the three faces of a wall end, an angle readout drawn on the ground like the distance guides, a gold ghost while snapped, and a toggleable tools panel starting with a "lock top height" mode. Each hand-test after that surfaced the next problem, and the session followed the pain: split ghosts, stepped gaps, the reach-back's obsolescence, height mismatches, curved-joint daylight, and finally the offset-corner tie-in — which ended as the tangent-matched connector.

## What we did

- **Exact end snapping** (`TryEndSnap` in `GridPlacementSystem.cs`): every wall end offers four exact points — the endpoint, the end-cap center (pushed half a thickness past the face so a stub stands beside the cap instead of straddling it), and the two flank points on the face planes. Nearest within `endpointSnapRadius` (0.75) wins; applies to the hover point **and the drag's far end** (loop closing); Alt frees. The endpoint candidate shows the solid start marker — a carved stub there trims to nothing.
- **Angle guide**: while dragging from a snapped anchor, a 24-segment arc (LineRenderer, 1.5m radius, terrain-following) plus an IMGUI degree label spans from the host's back-bearing to the drag bearing — 90° reads "square corner," 180° reads "continuation." Suppressed when the connector is tangent-tied (no corner exists).
- **Gold snap tint** (`snapColor`): the ghost runs gold while *any* snap decides its position — end snaps, flank snaps, drag-end snaps, course hover — and blue otherwise. Snapped ghosts also adopt the host's rotation (flank snaps lie flush along the wall; end snaps stand perpendicular, since a parallel stub would half-bury and flag red).
- **Options panel + lock-top mode** (`BuildMenu.CreateOptionsPanel`/`CreateToggleRow`, `SplineWall.fixedTopY`): a toggle list above the toolbar; the first toggle switches walls from constant-height (top rides the terrain) to locked-top (every section's top at one absolute height, bottom follows the ground down — a level parapet). Sentinel is `float.NegativeInfinity`, not NaN — NaN breaks tuple-equality preview keys and causes per-frame rebuild churn.
- **Split-ghost fix**: the cap snap candidate originally sat *on* the end face; a perpendicular stub there straddled the host and the junction band carved it in two. Moved out half a thickness; the endpoint candidate's invisible-stub case got the solid marker instead.
- **Collinear fuse fix** (the stepped-gap bug): end-on burials couldn't form junction bands under the 10° angle gate, so whole buried sections were occupancy-dropped and walls started up to 0.5m past the host's cap. `ScanLane` now applies the gate only where the wall's *body* overlaps a host (`RequireAngleAt`); end-extension overshoots band at any angle and trim flush at the host's cap. Cap-snap continuations, endpoint chains, and gap-closing all measure flush (cap + 1.5cm inset).
- **Reach-back removed** (user call: snapping made it obsolete): `EndExtension` probes shrank from 1.45m to 0.6m — contact only. A snapped or touching end still fuses; a visible gap is never bridged. The old "1m gap = deliberate separator" rule went with it.
- **Smooth + snap-matched height**: Ctrl+scroll now slides height continuously (`heightScrollRate` 0.25m/notch, raw ±120 Windows deltas normalized; `heightMultiplierStep` deleted). A snapped ghost adopts the snapped wall's exact height via `EffectiveWallHeight` (anchor snap > drag-end snap > hover snap > scrolled); the HUD readout shows it live. **Locked-top hosts match by top elevation** (`SnapHeightOf` = `fixedTopY` − ground at the snap; stored height means nothing on a slope), so extending a parapet with the lock on continues the exact level top. **Ctrl+scroll overrides the match** (`heightOverride`, re-arms when the drag ends or the hover leaves the snap).
- **Curvature-aware cut sinking** (`FaceRecession` in `SplineWall.cs`): a cut plane raycast off a curved host is only the tangent at one point — on the convex side the real face bows away and a flat cap sat proud by the sagitta (the daylight wedge in the user's screenshot). The plane now sinks by the deepest recession of the host's **analytic** face (its Bézier offset by half thickness) across the cut's lateral span, capped at 0.25m; flat hosts measure zero. Grazes get the same treatment along the band. Chosen over the user's filler-mesh idea: no new object lifecycle, no UV seams, previews get it free.
- **Tangent-tied connectors** (the user's own idea, verbatim: "the game should figure out the appropriate curvature"): when both drag ends snap to wall ends, `TryTangentControl` intersects the two snaps' departure rays (`EndSnap.outDir` — outward for endpoint/cap, sideways for flanks) into the quadratic's control point, decomposed into the (bulge, apexT) the pipeline already speaks. The connector leaves one host and arrives at the other straight-on, so both joints are the now-flush collinear fuse — the corner artifacts stop existing rather than getting patched. Shift keeps the drag straight; parallel runs (an S) and diverging tangents fall back to straight.
- **Three junction/occupancy fixes the connector forced**: (1) pieces cut by a band pull their occupancy box back 0.3m from the cut (`AddSpecIfClear` clearArc range) — the deliberate burial read as a near-parallel duplicate and dropped the piece; (2) only **gate-qualified** bands get the stay-open hysteresis — a parallel band opened in the gate-free end zone died at the zone boundary, closing a refusal-bypass *introduced by this session's own gap fix* (a redraw entering through a cap silently swallowed the whole wall instead of showing red); (3) the angle gate re-judges near-threshold calls against the host's **local tangents** — a tight curve's sections turn 20°+ each, enough for a wall to read as crossing its own redraw.
- All of it verified programmatically in play mode each round; final suite: connector flush both ends + zero blocked, exact redraw 9/9 red, genuine crossing through the curved connector 6/6 cut, cap fuse flush, gap preserved, perpendicular junctions intact, inside/cap-entry redraws refused.

## Decisions & trade-offs

- **Adaptive burial over filler mesh** for curved joints: the system already hides seams by interpenetration (the 1.5cm inset); making the inset curvature-aware is a few lines in one place versus a third geometry type threaded through commit/preview/delete/rebuild with mismatched UVs. Filler mesh stays the right tool only for tight towers (r < ~2m) where the 0.25m sink cap runs out.
- **Tangent-tie over corner patching**: a curved connector makes both joints collinear fuses — the two-face corner problem is sidestepped, not solved. Deliberate sharp corners still want the two-plane clip someday.
- **Height is the stepping exception now**: bulge and bases stay stepped; height scrolls smoothly because alignment comes from snap-matching instead of shared increments. CLAUDE.md philosophy line updated accordingly.
- **Match by top elevation for locked-top hosts** — their stored height is just what was scrolled at commit; the profile on a slope is `fixedTopY − ground`.
- **Scroll beats snap, snap beats scroll's memory**: an explicit Ctrl+scroll during a snap context overrides the match until that context ends, then matching re-arms. Alt remains the full escape hatch.
- **Measure the analytic curve, not the colliders**: wall sections use rotated *box* colliders — a curved wall's collision surface has cracks and interior caps between boxes. Anything that needs the true face must evaluate the Bézier.

## Dead ends

- **Raycast-based `FaceRecession`**: lateral rays fell into the crack between section box colliders and hit an angled interior cap — 46cm of phantom recession. Replaced the same hour by the analytic version.
- **Control-polygon curve-length estimate**: overestimated length by ~35%, so recession samples stopped short of the cap corners and left them 1mm proud. Fixed with (chord + polygon) / 2 plus a 1.5× reach pad — overshoot buries a few harmless millimeters deeper; undershoot is the bug.
- **Synthetic corner probes for oblique verification**: computed "corner" points that weren't on the actual clipped mesh reported +12.9cm of daylight that didn't exist. Real mesh vertices on the cut planes became the assertion.

## Bugs hit

- **Split gold ghost** at end-face snap; **invisible ghost** at endpoint snap (carved stub trims to zero pieces) — both from candidates interacting with the band machinery.
- **Stepped gaps** after cap-snap extension — the headline fix; also cured a pre-existing endpoint-chain gap.
- **Locked-top hosts ignored by the height match** (user report): stored height ≠ slope profile; also found the degenerate case where terrain rises above a locked top (wall has no sections there; matched height clamps to 0.25m).
- **First connector piece dropped as a duplicate**: short trimmed piece's occupancy box reached 1cm into the deliberate burial; the straight fuse had been passing the same check by a 12cm accident.
- **Redraw-through-cap vanish**: introduced by this session's own hysteresis rule, caught because a test asserted red and got zero specs. The one surviving sliver (between two bands, occupancy-exempt) was the tell.
- **Tight-curve self-crossing**: box-yaw angle test crossed the 10° gate against the redraw's own sections.
- **Test-scene pollution, twice**: leftover walls from earlier execute_code runs made a later build read fully-blocked (NRE on zero sections), and a cleanup destroyed the connector before its redraw test — diagnose by listing `SplineWall.All` before trusting any occupancy result.
